## Durable cautions

- A green scripted suite does not prove model stopping, tool selection, streaming, or
  browser-visible behavior. Verify those paths directly.
- When a tool refuses an action, return the actionable alternative or missing identifiers;
  an identical refusal can cause the model to retry until its cap.
- Keep model work outside the long-held conversation lock. Persisted thread events may
  take the lock briefly to allocate their ordered sequence.
- Do not add a second persona pipeline to satisfy old copy. Use the existing candidate
  profiler, coordinator tools, and target-assessment specialists at their actual seams.
- Do not preserve tests that assert superseded behavior. Replace them with coverage for
  the current contract, while retaining evidence and safety gates.
- Never run mutation-heavy trace scripts against the primary local database.

## Product invariant

The assistant may autonomously search, compare, explain, and draft within the candidate's
job hunt. Resume claims and edits must remain supported by the candidate's resume and the
job evidence. The candidate can interrupt, change direction, ask why, or ask the team to
finish the work without losing accepted evidence or pending edits.
